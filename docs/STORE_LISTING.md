# Store listing draft

**App name:** Bible Door

**Short description:** Read your Bible offline, explore sections, and compare translations.

**Full description:**

Open a door to Scripture with Bible Door from Freely-Given.org.

Enter a key shared by your Bible publisher or translator, or import a Bible Door
package. Download the books you need and read them wherever you are, even offline.
You can also start with a freely available Bible from the Library.

Read in context. Bible Door follows the translation's sections and paragraphs,
keeping verse numbers quiet until you choose to show them. Explore introductions,
section headings, footnotes and cross-reference notes without losing your place.

Read two passages together. Open two translations, or two places in the same
Bible. Link their references and scrolling, or navigate each independently.
Translations with different verse numbering may not align automatically.

Find and return. Search words and phrases in downloaded books, paste a passage
reference, and bookmark sections to revisit later.

Make reading comfortable with adjustable text size, serif or sans serif type,
light and dark themes, and optional red letters where supported by the translation.

Bible Door has no advertisements, analytics or account requirement. Reading
preferences and bookmarks stay on your device. Downloads connect to
Freely-Given.org, whose server may keep normal access logs.

An internet connection is needed to download new books. You can import a package
without a connection. Sharing downloaded Bible packages is available when the
publisher permits it. No Bible translation is bundled with the app.

**Suggested category:** Books & Reference

**Reviewer instructions:** Open Library and choose World English Bible, or enter
`webbe-123-456`. No account, payment or private credentials are needed. The first
book downloads; use the Bible menu to download the remaining books for offline use.

**Release notes:** First Flutter release: key-based Bible downloads, offline
section reading, two reading panes, search, bookmarks, reading preferences, and
Bible Door package import/export.

The publisher must supply the actual support email, privacy-policy URL, target
audience and content-rating answers in Play Console.
